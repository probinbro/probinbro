## About

I build websites for people who need the thing to actually work, not just look good in a mockup.

Everything here is hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No page builders, no dragged-and-dropped templates. If a client needs a booking flow, a pricing calculator or a product filter, I build it rather than bolt on a plugin and hope.

Most of what you see below is live right now. Click through and poke at it.
